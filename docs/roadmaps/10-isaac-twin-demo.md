# Phase 10: the cart in the NTU twin on Isaac Sim, a demo a person can run

Started 2026-11-07. The goal is a demo of the NTU campus twin on Isaac Sim. The golf
cart drives one or two turns in front of 學新館. The cart's own Autoware drives it,
and Isaac Sim's editor shows the run. The work is in the setup and the start-up: one
person, following one page, brings everything up and starts the drive.

The twin and the simulator side come from
[scenario-world-bridge](https://github.com/NEWSLabNTU/scenario-world-bridge) (swb):
- the twin is its roadmap 059, the NTU campus from the vendor's scan as an Isaac
  world, proven over 116 legs of r01/r02 with a Tesla Model 3 and Autoware;
- swb's `autoware_isaac_bridge` (aib) builds the PhysX vehicle and its sensors, and
  plays the vehicle interface.

This repository adds what makes the cart the ego:
- its body;
- its sensor rig;
- the glue that runs the cart's own launch against the simulator;
- the demo recipes.

## Decisions (2026-11-07)

- **The cart's own Autoware**, not swb's simulation profile. `golfcart_launch`'s
  logging-simulation mode already runs with drivers off and sim time. Isaac
  publishes the cart's real topic names; aib plays the vehicle interface
  (`/vehicle/status/*` out, `/control/command/*` in).
- **A low-poly body built from photos**, by a script. The photos are of the cart as
  it stands in 2026: rounded white hood, windshield, canopy and roof rack, the
  VLP-32C mast, the front deck with the Falcon over the ZED X, the four-door cargo
  box, the bench seat. The script sizes everything to `vehicle_info.param.yaml`.

## What the cart carries, and how Isaac gives it

Sensor poses are taken from `golfcart_sensor_kit_description` (`sensors_calibration.yaml`,
`sensor_kit_calibration.yaml`). Topics are the drivers' names.

| Sensor | Real topic | In Isaac |
|---|---|---|
| Velodyne VLP-32C, roof | `/sensing/lidar/vlp32/velodyne_points` (PointXYZIRCAEDT, frame `velodyne`, 10 Hz) | RTX lidar, 32 channels, VLP-32C elevation table (no stock Isaac profile: written here) |
| Seyond Falcon, front deck | `/sensing/lidar/falcon/iv_points` (PointXYZIRC, frame `seyond`, 10 Hz) | RTX lidar, a 120 x 25 deg solid-state pattern (written here) |
| ZED X, front deck | `/sensing/camera/zed/rgb/color/rect/image`, 1920x1200, and `/sensing/camera/zed/imu/data` | RTX camera (the left lens); the IMU from the body |
| GMSL left, right, rear | `/sensing/camera/{left,right,rear}/image_raw/compressed`, 1920x1280 | RTX cameras with the calibration's intrinsics; lower rate for the demo |
| Xsens IMU | `/sensing/imu/xsens/imu_raw` | the body's IMU. The cart's `IMU_SOURCE=zed` picks the ZED's |
| u-blox F9P | `/sensing/gnss/ublox/nav_sat_fix`, 1 Hz | aib's GNSS from the twin's MGRS frame |
| VCU | `/vehicle/status/*`, `/control/command/*` | aib's vehicle interface |

Autoware uses the two lidars, concatenated, for NDT, plus the IMU and wheel twist.
The cameras are for the people watching: RViz and the editor.

## Risks

- **學新館 is at the twin's east edge** (OSM 25.01909 N, 121.54361 E = map x 53048,
  y 67852). The lanes end at x 53038, and east of x ~53030 the scan is thin. The
  route must stay on the scanned roads.
- **GPU.** Isaac renders the editor and five cameras, and cuda_ndt shares the GPU.
  118 has two RTX 4090s: Isaac goes on one, Autoware's CUDA on the other.
- **The cart's config assumes hardware** in places:
  - `use_sim_time` is fixed false in `golfcart.launch.yaml`; logging_simulation sets
    it true.
  - The planning presets are not forwarded in logging_simulation.
  - Perception defaults to on there.
  T5 settles each one.
